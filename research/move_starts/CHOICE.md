# Choice on the development years

From `dev.txt` (2013-2020; the combined score judged on 2019-2020), the candidates with the
highest calendar-time t of net returns, as the protocol requires:

- **Buy: PBUY:63**, buying at the next open after a promoter or promoter-group open-market
  purchase is published, held 63 sessions: +0.81% a trade over the market after costs,
  +1.11% a month in calendar time, t 1.37 (758 trades). This is close to the variant
  "every purchase, 63 sessions" of `research/promoter` (development t 1.38), whose
  confirmation was not run there because another variant was chosen; so this confirmation
  is not independent of that study's data.
- **Sell: UP5:21**, selling futures at the next open after a stock with futures closes 5%
  or more above the market, held 21 sessions: +0.55% a trade after costs, +0.69% a month,
  t 2.75 (3,359 trades).

No buy candidate reached t 2. The combined buy score lost money on 2019-2020 (-1.09% a
month, 78 trades). The combined sell score made +16.7% a month on 2019-2020 (t 2.19) but
from only 31 trades in 13 months, short of UP5:21's t.

The confirmation (2021 to October 2026) is run once for these two.
