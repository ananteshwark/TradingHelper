# Choice on the development years

From `dev.txt` (2013-2020), the rule and holding period with the highest calendar-time t of
net returns, as the protocol requires:

- **R4:63, accumulation at the lows:** the stock fell 30% or more within six months, is
  within 15% of its 250-session low, its delivery value over the last 20 sessions is at
  least twice its average over the 120 sessions before, and the price is no lower than 20
  sessions ago. Bought at the next open and held 63 sessions: +2.23% a trade over the
  market after costs, +3.53% a month in calendar time, t 1.55 (131 trades in 63 months),
  positive in 4 of 8 years.

It is below t 2 even on the years it was chosen on. Every other rule lost money there:
the 150-session breakout (R1), the 25% recovery off the low (R2), the tight-base breakout
(R5) and the rebound state (R6) at both holding periods; capitulation then a higher low
(R3) was flat at 63 sessions on 74 trades and lost 12% a trade at 126.

The confirmation (2021 to October 2026) is run once for R4:63.
